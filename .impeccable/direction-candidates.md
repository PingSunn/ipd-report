# Grounded directions

1. Ward census ledger: ruled daily totals and grouped numeric columns.
2. Nursing observation chart: time-axis records with a stable category legend.
3. Bed allocation board: occupancy and availability as the organizing structure.
4. Shift calendar: days and three shifts provide primary navigation.
5. Hospital quality report: comparable period totals and workload trends.
6. Nursing handover strip: one shift summary followed by entry and copy actions.
7. Duty roster: compact shift rows with a persistent current-shift marker.

Assigned direction: 6, nursing handover strip. Each direction preserves the census domain and supports mobile and desktop.
